This folder was the first iteration of what would become of the 'google_maps_api_scripts' folder, kept here for history sake.

This folder would list out all of the Seattle Points of interests (SeattlePOI.csv), any and all locations
and their Coordinates. The reason the coordinates are included id because thats how the Google Maps API handles
their queries in finding out the travel time between two different places.

The 161 SeattlePOI would be narrowed down by doing hundreds of queries to find the 'perfect' routes to train and test upon.

You can mostly ignore this folder.
